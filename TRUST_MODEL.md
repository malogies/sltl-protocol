# SLTL Trust Model

Trust is established externally via **SLTL Trust** — an Alpha91 brand operating the sole Trust Authority for the SLTL protocol at `sltltrust.com`, with the public link-verification endpoint at `sltl.global`.

SLTL Trust is the sole Trust Authority for the SLTL protocol; no other party may issue, vet, suspend, or revoke SLTL Trusted seals or represent links as SLTL Trusted.

Implementations of this specification MUST NOT operate as an SLTL trust authority.
