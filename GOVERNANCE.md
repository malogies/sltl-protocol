# SLTL Governance Model

The SLTL protocol is an open specification maintained by the SLTL Maintainers.
Changes are introduced through versioned releases.

The SLTL Maintainers operate under **SLTL Trust**, an Alpha91 brand. Trust authority decisions (issuer verification, seal issuance, revocation) are not part of the protocol governance process and are made solely by SLTL Trust.
